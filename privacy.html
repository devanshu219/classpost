ClassPost privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

What the app accesses

  • Your YouTube account (Google permission "Manage your YouTube account"): only to upload your class recordings as Unlisted videos, list your own playlists, create a playlist when you ask, and add videos to your playlists.
  • Your calendar (only if you choose it): read-only, to work out which class a recording belongs to.
  • Your Zoom recordings folder: read-only, to find finished recordings. The app never deletes or changes a recording.

Where your data goes

Everything stays on your Mac. The app talks only to Google's sign-in and YouTube servers, and only to do the actions above. Nothing is sent to the app's maker or to anyone else. There are no analytics, no tracking and no advertising.

Storage

Your Google sign-in is kept in your Mac's Keychain on that Mac only. A small record of which recordings were uploaded is kept in the app's own folder on your Mac.

Removing access

In the app, choose Settings, then Account, then Sign out. This removes access at Google and deletes the sign-in from your Mac. You can also remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. "Remove all app data" in Settings, then Help, deletes everything the app stored.

Google API Services

ClassPost's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
